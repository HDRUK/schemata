## Changes from 2.2 -> 2.3

### Added

- **`crocod`** *(optional)*: Trial case study content that has no home in the core GWDM sections. Present only on records whose required.recordType is caseStudy. Title, acronym, participant count, trial years and participant locations are stored in summary, provenance.temporal and coverage instead.
- **`crocod.background`** *(optional)*: Clinical and research background to the trial, in full.
- **`crocod.governance`** *(optional)*: Who sponsored, funded, approved and led the trial.
- **`crocod.governance.approvingBody`** *(optional)*: Body that approved access to the data.
- **`crocod.governance.contactPoints`** *(optional)*: Email addresses for contacting the trial team.
- **`crocod.governance.funder`** *(optional)*: Funder of the trial, in full.
- **`crocod.governance.principalInvestigator`** *(optional)*: Name of the trial's principal investigator.
- **`crocod.governance.sponsor`** *(optional)*: Sponsor of the trial.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData`** *(optional)*: How the trial used health systems data (HSD), and which data it applied for.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.dataProvidersAppliedTo`** *(optional)*: Data custodians the trial applied to for data, Gateway or not.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.dataProvidersAppliedTo.gatewayId`** *(optional)*: Identifier of the entity on the Gateway, as text, if it is listed there. Leave empty for entities that are not on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.dataProvidersAppliedTo.title`**: Name of the entity, shown whether or not it is listed on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.dataProvidersAppliedTo.url`** *(optional)*: Link to the entity, for entities that are not on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.datasetsAccessed`** *(optional)*: Datasets the trial accessed, Gateway or not.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.datasetsAccessed.gatewayId`** *(optional)*: Identifier of the entity on the Gateway, as text, if it is listed there. Leave empty for entities that are not on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.datasetsAccessed.title`**: Name of the entity, shown whether or not it is listed on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.datasetsAccessed.url`** *(optional)*: Link to the entity, for entities that are not on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.usedFor`** *(optional)*: What the trial used health systems data for.
- **`crocod.healthSystemsData.yearFirstAppliedFor`** *(optional)*: Year the trial first applied for health systems data.
- **`crocod.laySummary`** *(optional)*: Plain-language summary of the trial, in full.
- **`crocod.learnings`** *(optional)*: What the trial learned from using health systems data, by area.
- **`crocod.learnings.additionalInsights`** *(optional)*: Any further insights from the trial.
- **`crocod.learnings.outcomeAscertainment`** *(optional)*: Learnings about using health systems data for outcome ascertainment.
- **`crocod.learnings.outcomeAscertainment.content`** *(optional)*: The learnings in full. May include formatting, block quotes and attributed quotes.
- **`crocod.learnings.outcomeAscertainment.summary`** *(optional)*: One-line summary of the learnings, displayed as a subheading.
- **`crocod.learnings.outcomeAscertainment.tags`** *(optional)*: Short statements, displayed as ticked boxes.
- **`crocod.learnings.patientPublicInvolvement`** *(optional)*: Learnings about patient and public involvement.
- **`crocod.learnings.patientPublicInvolvement.content`** *(optional)*: The learnings in full. May include formatting, block quotes and attributed quotes.
- **`crocod.learnings.patientPublicInvolvement.summary`** *(optional)*: One-line summary of the learnings, displayed as a subheading.
- **`crocod.learnings.patientPublicInvolvement.tags`** *(optional)*: Short statements, displayed as ticked boxes.
- **`crocod.learnings.pharmacovigilance`** *(optional)*: Learnings about using health systems data for pharmacovigilance.
- **`crocod.learnings.pharmacovigilance.content`** *(optional)*: The learnings in full. May include formatting, block quotes and attributed quotes.
- **`crocod.learnings.pharmacovigilance.summary`** *(optional)*: One-line summary of the learnings, displayed as a subheading.
- **`crocod.learnings.pharmacovigilance.tags`** *(optional)*: Short statements, displayed as ticked boxes.
- **`crocod.learnings.recruitment`** *(optional)*: Learnings about using health systems data for recruitment.
- **`crocod.learnings.recruitment.content`** *(optional)*: The learnings in full. May include formatting, block quotes and attributed quotes.
- **`crocod.learnings.recruitment.summary`** *(optional)*: One-line summary of the learnings, displayed as a subheading.
- **`crocod.learnings.recruitment.tags`** *(optional)*: Short statements, displayed as ticked boxes.
- **`crocod.publications`** *(optional)*: Publications from the trial, Gateway or not.
- **`crocod.publications.gatewayId`** *(optional)*: Identifier of the entity on the Gateway, as text, if it is listed there. Leave empty for entities that are not on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.publications.title`**: Name of the entity, shown whether or not it is listed on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.publications.url`** *(optional)*: Link to the entity, for entities that are not on the Gateway.
- **`crocod.readMore`** *(optional)*: Further documents or links about the trial.
- **`crocod.therapeuticArea`** *(optional)*: Disease or clinical area the trial addresses.
- **`crocod.trialDesign`** *(optional)*: How the trial was designed and registered.
- **`crocod.trialDesign.categories`** *(optional)*: Design classification of the trial, for example masking, control and phase.
- **`crocod.trialDesign.designDescription`** *(optional)*: Description of the trial design.
- **`crocod.trialDesign.registration`** *(optional)*: Registry identifiers for the trial.
- **`crocod.trialStatus`**: Current status of the trial.
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf`** *(optional)*: The study this dataset is a wave or sub-study of. Which wave it is lives in population.wave.
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf.pid`** *(optional)*: Persistent identifier of a dataset
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf.title`** *(optional)*: Title of a dataset
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf.url`** *(optional)*: Url of a dataset
- **`population`** *(optional)*: Population-research detail: study design, wave, recruitment, follow-up and eligibility.
- **`population.eligibility`** *(optional)*: Who could take part in the study.
- **`population.eligibility.exclusionCriteria`** *(optional)*: Criteria that excluded a participant from taking part.
- **`population.eligibility.inclusionCriteria`** *(optional)*: Criteria a participant had to meet to take part.
- **`population.followUpSchedule`** *(optional)*: Whether participants are actively re-contacted, and how often. This is different from coverage.followUp, which records how long, in total, a participant appears in the dataset.
- **`population.followUpSchedule.frequency`**: How often participants are re-contacted. Select 'No scheduled follow-up' if they are not re-contacted on a schedule.
- **`population.followUpSchedule.note`** *(optional)*: Any further detail on the follow-up schedule.
- **`population.recruitment`** *(optional)*: How and when participants were recruited.
- **`population.recruitment.endDate`** *(optional)*: Date recruitment ended. Leave empty if recruitment is ongoing.
- **`population.recruitment.samplingMethod`** *(optional)*: How participants were identified and approached.
- **`population.recruitment.startDate`** *(optional)*: Date recruitment began.
- **`population.recruitment.status`** *(optional)*: Whether the study is currently recruiting.
- **`population.studyDesign`** *(optional)*: Characteristics of the study design. Leave a value empty if it is not known; false means the study is known not to have that characteristic.
- **`population.studyDesign.isClinicalBased`** *(optional)*: Whether participants are recruited through a clinical setting rather than from the general population.
- **`population.studyDesign.isFamilyBased`** *(optional)*: Whether participants are recruited as families (related individuals) rather than as unrelated individuals.
- **`population.studyDesign.isLongitudinal`** *(optional)*: Whether the study follows the same participants over time.
- **`population.wave`** *(optional)*: Which wave or sub-study of the parent study this dataset represents.
- **`population.wave.label`** *(optional)*: Human-readable name for this wave.
- **`population.wave.waveId`**: Identifier for this wave, unique within the parent study.
- **`population.wave.waveNumber`** *(optional)*: Position of this contact wave within the parent study, starting from 1. Leave empty for sub-studies.
- **`population.wave.waveType`**: Whether this is a contact wave (participants were contacted and data collected) or a sub-study (further analysis, such as an assay, of data or samples collected in an earlier wave).
- **`required.recordType`** *(optional)*: What kind of record this is. Records without a type are datasets.
