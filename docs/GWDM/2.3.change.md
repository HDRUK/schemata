## Changes from 2.2 -> 2.3

### Added

- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf`** *(optional)*: The study this dataset is a wave or sub-study of. Which wave it is lives in population.wave.
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf.pid`** *(optional)*: Persistent identifier of a dataset
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf.title`** *(optional)*: Title of a dataset
- **`linkage.datasetLinkage.waveOf.url`** *(optional)*: Url of a dataset
- **`population`** *(optional)*: Population-research detail: study design, wave, recruitment, follow-up and eligibility.
- **`population.eligibility`** *(optional)*: Who could take part in the study.
- **`population.eligibility.exclusionCriteria`** *(optional)*: Criteria that excluded a participant from taking part.
- **`population.eligibility.inclusionCriteria`** *(optional)*: Criteria a participant had to meet to take part.
- **`population.followUpSchedule`** *(optional)*: Whether participants are actively re-contacted, and how often. This is different from coverage.followUp, which records how long, in total, a participant appears in the dataset.
- **`population.followUpSchedule.frequency`**: How often participants are re-contacted. Select 'No scheduled follow-up' if they are not re-contacted on a schedule.
- **`population.followUpSchedule.note`** *(optional)*: Any further detail on the follow-up schedule.
- **`population.recruitment`** *(optional)*: How and when participants were recruited.
- **`population.recruitment.endDate`** *(optional)*: Date recruitment ended. Leave empty if recruitment is ongoing.
- **`population.recruitment.samplingMethod`** *(optional)*: How participants were identified and approached.
- **`population.recruitment.startDate`** *(optional)*: Date recruitment began.
- **`population.recruitment.status`** *(optional)*: Whether the study is currently recruiting.
- **`population.studyDesign`** *(optional)*: Characteristics of the study design. Leave a value empty if it is not known; false means the study is known not to have that characteristic.
- **`population.studyDesign.isClinicalBased`** *(optional)*: Whether participants are recruited through a clinical setting rather than from the general population.
- **`population.studyDesign.isFamilyBased`** *(optional)*: Whether participants are recruited as families (related individuals) rather than as unrelated individuals.
- **`population.studyDesign.isLongitudinal`** *(optional)*: Whether the study follows the same participants over time.
- **`population.wave`** *(optional)*: Which wave or sub-study of the parent study this dataset represents.
- **`population.wave.label`** *(optional)*: Human-readable name for this wave.
- **`population.wave.waveId`**: Identifier for this wave, unique within the parent study.
- **`population.wave.waveNumber`** *(optional)*: Position of this contact wave within the parent study, starting from 1. Leave empty for sub-studies.
- **`population.wave.waveType`**: Whether this is a contact wave (participants were contacted and data collected) or a sub-study (further analysis, such as an assay, of data or samples collected in an earlier wave).
